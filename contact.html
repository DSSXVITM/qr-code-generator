Frequently asked questions

Is my data uploaded? No. All QR generation happens in your browser; nothing is sent to our servers. Saved designs live only in your local browser storage.

Why is my QR not scanning? Increase error correction to H, keep a clear quiet zone, and check contrast. You can verify a scan directly in QRVolt before downloading.

Can I use the QR codes commercially? Yes. Codes you generate are yours to use; we only ask that linked content follows the law and our Terms.

Do you offer dynamic QR codes? Dynamic codes need a server to host the redirect. QRVolt is fully offline by design, so it produces static codes — which is also why your data stays private.
