QRVolt Updates

A running log of what we’ve added to QRVolt. Everything runs 100% in your browser — no account, no uploads.

Latest

Install it. Share it. Print it right.

  • Installable app (PWA) — add QRVolt to your home screen / desktop and it keeps working with zero internet, thanks to a built-in service worker.
  • Share design as a link — the new “Share design” button packs your entire design into the URL, so a colleague can open and tweak the exact same QR.
  • Caption styles — render your label as plain text or as a “SCAN ME” pill button baked into the image.
  • Print-size hint — a badge now shows the minimum recommended physical size for reliable scanning.
  • Ctrl/Cmd + S downloads your QR as PNG instantly.
  • ZIP batch export — Multi-URL and Social batches can be downloaded as one .zip archive of PNGs.
Previously

Scan & read QR codes

  • Scan & Read QR — upload a screenshot/photo or use your camera to decode any QR, then copy the text, open the link, or load it back into the generator.
  • New content types: WhatsApp, PayPal, Crypto (BTC/ETH/LTC/DOGE/DASH), PDF (link to a PDF), App (Google Play / App Store), Multi-URL and Social batch generators that create a separate QR for each link.
Design & branding

Make it yours

  • Caption — add text above or below the code (great for print).
  • QRVolt logo on top of the QR, plus a center logo enabled by default.
  • Module shapes: Square, Rounded, Dots, Classy, Bars, Diamond.
  • 3-stop gradients (linear/radial) and 8 colour palettes.
  • Quiet-zone slider and a full Reset all button.
Export & quality

Sharper, more formats

  • Resolution up to 2048 px and 8× PNG scale.
  • Formats: PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG, PDF, Print, Copy to clipboard.
  • Scan-verification hint so the code you save is the code that scans.
Trust & compliance

AdSense-ready

  • Full legal & info set: Privacy, Terms, About, Guide, Contact and this Updates page.
  • Cookie consent banner with Google AdSense / DoubleClick disclosure (ads load only after consent).
  • robots.txt + sitemap.xml, meta descriptions on every page, and a Blog Wi-Fi QR QR with logo Business card QR article.
