QR code for business cards

Turn a scan into a saved contact — no typing, no typos.

A vCard QR code on your business card lets someone save your details to their phone contacts with one scan. It stores name, phone, email, company, website and more — far faster than manual entry.

How to create it

  1. Open QRVolt and choose the Contact (vCard) tab.
  2. Fill name, organisation, phone, email, website — only what you want to share.
  3. Generate and export as PNG, SVG or PDF.
  4. Print it small but clear on the card, with a quiet zone around it.

Why it works

Scannable contact codes remove friction at networking events. Pair it with a logo for brand recall, and always verify the scan on a phone before a big print run.
