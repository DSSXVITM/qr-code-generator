About QRVolt

QRVolt is a free, fast, and private QR code generator. Everything runs directly in your browser — no uploads, no tracking of your content, no server round-trips. Type a URL, Wi-Fi credential, contact card, or any text, style it the way you like, and export it as PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG, or PDF.

Why people use QRVolt

100% offline. Your data never leaves the device.
Many formats. PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG, PDF, and print.
Rich design. Gradients, palettes, logos, and multiple module shapes.
Flexible content. URL, text, Wi-Fi, email, vCard, SMS, calls, geo, events.
Instant preview. Live canvas with scan-verification hints.
No sign-up. Just open and generate.

How it works

QRVolt includes a self-contained implementation of the QR code standard (ISO/IEC 18004). When you enter content, it is encoded into a matrix in your browser and rendered to a canvas or SVG you can download. Saved designs and history are stored only in your local browser.
